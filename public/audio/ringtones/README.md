## Notes

- Keep all ringtone assets in `public/audio/ringtones/`.
- Use MP3 files only.
- Do not rename these files after they are wired into the ringtone picker; the app can refer to them by their public paths, for example `/audio/ringtones/ringtone-01.mp3`.
- The app's ringtone player should repeat the selected file until the call is answered, declined, cancelled, or times out.
- Repeating a 1-second clip does not create additional audio content. Very short clips may sound repetitive or have an audible seam; prefer a clean loop point and a short fade at the end/start where appropriate.
- Only add audio that is licensed for redistribution and use in the SociaLens app.

The MP3 files themselves are not included yet. Add the 50 files with the exact names above.
